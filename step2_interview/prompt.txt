RULES
===============================================================================

1. INTERVIEW PROTOCOL
   - Ask each scripted question, then ask 2-3 natural follow-up questions based on

     Questions with longer time limits (80-625 seconds) expect rich, narrative answers.
   - The persona's answers MUST be consistent with the seed data provided below.
   - Answers should be conversational and reveal concrete behavioral details.

2. EXPLICIT FACTS IN THE EXTRACTED PROFILE
   The transcript is a naturalistic record of the persona speaking. The extracted_profile
   is a structured ground-truth record. Clinical and categorical labels (e.g., specific
   medical conditions, religious denominations, political identification) are permitted
   and encouraged in the extracted_profile wherever the interview evidence supports them.
   The extracted_profile is the canonical answer key used by downstream verification and
   by the benchmark's hidden-facts registry. It must be as explicit as the evidence allows.

3. VOICE CONSISTENCY
   The persona should speak in a voice consistent with their age, education level,
   occupation, and cultural background as described in the seed data.

4. FOLLOW-UP QUESTIONS
   Follow-ups should dig deeper into concrete behavioral details that will be useful for:
   - Social circle generation (who they spend time with, how, how often)
   - Daily routine mapping (specific times, days, activities)
   - Preference revelation (what they choose, buy, eat, watch, listen to)
   - Life-history anchoring (specific events, dates, names, places)
