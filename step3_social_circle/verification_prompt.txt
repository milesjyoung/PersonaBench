SYSTEM:

You are verifying the social circle against the interview transcript and the corrected
extracted profile. Your task has three purposes:

1. VERIFY the social circle across two directions (see VERIFICATION TASK).
2. RESOLVE inconsistencies using defined resolution actions.
3. CORRECT the social circle by applying resolutions and filling gaps, preserving
   entries that passed verification.

The corrected_social_circle produced here is the CANONICAL output used by all
downstream steps.

This prompt is self-contained. All required data is included below.

===============================================================================
INPUT DATA
===============================================================================

INTERVIEW TRANSCRIPT:
{{INSERT_TRANSCRIPT_JSON_HERE}}

CORRECTED EXTRACTED PROFILE (from the previous step's output):
{{INSERT_CORRECTED_EXTRACTED_PROFILE_JSON_HERE}}

DRAFT SOCIAL CIRCLE:
{{INSERT_SOCIAL_CIRCLE_JSON_HERE}}

===============================================================================
TERMINOLOGY
===============================================================================

Two concepts drive the verification output and they are independent:

- VERDICT (consistent | inconsistent): Describes whether the social circle entry
  AGREES or DISAGREES with a related claim in the interview or extracted profile.

- GAP (true | false): Describes whether a social circle claim introduces information
  absent from the interview and the extracted profile, OR whether the interview
  mentions a person or relationship absent from the social circle.

- UNSOURCED (true | false): Describes whether the `evidence_from_interview` field
  cites a Q## reference that does not actually support the claim.

===============================================================================
VERIFICATION TASK — TWO DIRECTIONS
===============================================================================

DIRECTION 1: SOCIAL CIRCLE ↔ INTERVIEW
For each of the five social circle members, compare their details (name,
relationship, demographics, personality, shared activities, recent topics,
evidence) against what is said in the interview transcript.

Produce observations checking:
- Whether the member was explicitly named in the interview or inferred.
- Whether the demographics match any interview references.
- Whether the `evidence_from_interview` Q## citations actually support the claim.
- Whether the interview mentions any close contact who is MISSING from the social
  circle (Direction 1 completeness).

DIRECTION 2: SOCIAL CIRCLE ↔ EXTRACTED PROFILE
For each of the five social circle members, compare their details against the
corrected extracted profile.

Produce observations checking:
- Whether shared activities align with the subject's `preferences` subcategories.
- Whether recent text topics align with the subject's documented interests.
- Whether the relationship dynamics are consistent with the relevant expert
  summary observations.
- Whether demographics and locations make sense given the subject's life context
  recorded in `preferences.home_and_lifestyle` and `preferences.social_and_relationships`.

===============================================================================
RESOLUTION ACTIONS
===============================================================================

For each observation with verdict = INCONSISTENT, assign one action:

- NO_CHANGE: The social circle entry is actually consistent on closer reading.
  Explain why.
- REPLACE_PERSON: The social circle entry must be replaced with a different
  person. Specify whom.
- PARTIAL_UPDATE: A specific field in the social circle entry must be revised.
  Specify the field and the new value.
- FLAG_FOR_REVIEW: The inconsistency is significant and requires human decision.

For each observation with gap = true or unsourced = true, assign one disposition:

- CARRY_FORWARD: Accept the entry as written with an inference-source note.
- REPLACE_PERSON: The entry is not supported and should be replaced.
- ENRICH_FROM_INTERVIEW: Add missing information from the interview to the entry.
- DROP_FROM_CIRCLE: Remove the person from the social circle.
- ADD_TO_CIRCLE: Add a missing contact identified in the interview to the circle.

The `resolution` field is populated ONLY when the action/disposition is anything
other than NO_CHANGE or CARRY_FORWARD. Otherwise it is null.

===============================================================================
CORRECTION TASK
===============================================================================

Produce a corrected_social_circle that:

1. APPLIES all resolutions determined above, except NO_CHANGE / CARRY_FORWARD.
2. PRESERVES members and fields that passed verification.
3. MAINTAINS a final count of exactly five members.

===============================================================================
OVERALL VERDICT
===============================================================================

Assign one of three overall verdicts:

- PASS: No INCONSISTENT observations with action ∈ {REPLACE_PERSON, PARTIAL_UPDATE,
  FLAG_FOR_REVIEW} AND no gap/unsourced observations with disposition ∈
  {REPLACE_PERSON, ENRICH_FROM_INTERVIEW, DROP_FROM_CIRCLE, ADD_TO_CIRCLE}.

- PASS_WITH_REVISIONS: Corrections were applied (any non-NO_CHANGE action or
  non-CARRY_FORWARD disposition), but no FLAG_FOR_REVIEW findings remain.

- FAIL: One or more observations have action = FLAG_FOR_REVIEW. Human decision
  is required before the corrected social circle is used downstream.
